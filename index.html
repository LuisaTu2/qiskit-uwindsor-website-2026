About Qiskit Fall Fest 2026

Discover the future of quantum computing.

What is Qiskit Fall Fest?

A global celebration

Fall Fest runs at universities around the world under the banner of the International Year of Quantum. Each host campus marks a century of quantum science and the work still ahead, and we join that programme here in Windsor.

Hosted in Windsor

The University of Windsor runs this edition alongside IBM Quantum and the Platform for Digital and Quantum Innovation (PINQ), bringing students, researchers, and mentors together for two days of building and learning.

And what is Qiskit?

Qiskit is IBM's open-source SDK for quantum computing. You use it to build and simulate quantum circuits on your own machine, then run the same circuits on real quantum hardware.

Hackathon

Hackathon experience

Creativity-first challenges, with support at every step.

This is a guided creativity sprint rather than an overnight coding marathon. You get prompts, quantum storylines, and the background you need to design something imaginative with your team.

  • Open to everyone

    No quantum background needed. Mentors are on-site and on Teams throughout.

  • All materials supplied

    Datasets, ideation templates, and worked examples, so your time goes into the idea.

  • Prizes included

    Top teams take home prizes and recognition from IBM Quantum, PINQ, and community partners.

Bring your curiosity, team up with people you haven't met, and present what you built on Day 2.

FAQ

Resources and FAQs

What to know before you arrive.

Install Qiskit and Python

Install Python 3.11 or newer and work through the Qiskit installation guide before the festival, so you can start the labs right away.

Bring your laptop

A laptop is required for the hackathon and the code labs. Wi-Fi, power, and mentors are on site to get you connected quickly.

Plan your meals

Refreshments are scheduled at set times. Outside those, bring your own food or use the many options in downtown Windsor during breaks.
